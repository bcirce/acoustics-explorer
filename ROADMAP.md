# Roadmap

What's being worked on, roughly in order. Plans change; this page follows them.

## Now

- **Own address.** Moving to **acousticsexplorer.com**, with sign-in emails sent from it.
- **Clearer privacy for list-keepers.** A one-time note when you save your first entry:
  your list lives on this device only.
- **Search engines.** Getting every entry properly listed in Google once the site is on its
  own address.

## Next

- **Finding more, automatically.** More official sources (societies, universities, job
  boards) and better reading of their pages, so fewer entries need a person to type them in.
- **Accessibility fixes**
  - Raise the contrast of dimmed sidebar text (chip counts, small labels) above 4.5:1.
  - Make map markers reachable one by one from the keyboard, and not colour-only.
  - A full walkthrough with NVDA on Windows (VoiceOver on iPhone is done).
- **Ended opportunities.** A shared link to an entry that has closed will show what's still
  open in that country, instead of a dead end.
- **Map credits.** Clearer credit for the map data (© CARTO, © OpenStreetMap contributors).

## Later

- **Regular data rounds.** New congress dates for 2027 as organisers publish them, and more
  job markets.
- **More coverage** where the map is still thin (parts of Europe, Latin America, Africa and
  Asia).
