# Roadmap

What's being worked on, roughly in order. Plans change; this page follows them.

## Now

- **Search engines.** Getting every entry properly listed in Google, now that the site lives
  at its own address.
- **Regular data rounds.** New congress dates for 2027 as organisers publish them, and more
  job markets.

## Next

- **Take your list with you.** My list lives in one browser today. Next: export it, or open
  it on another device through a link, still with no account.
- **A real choice on the first visit.** A small note: *this site remembers your settings on
  this device*, with a **Don't remember anything** option that keeps nothing at all.
- **Finding more, automatically.** More official sources (societies, universities, job
  boards) and better reading of their pages, so fewer entries need a person to type them in.
- **Accessibility fixes**
  - Make map markers reachable one by one from the keyboard, and not colour-only.
  - A full walkthrough with NVDA on Windows (VoiceOver on iPhone is done).
  - Darker selected chips in the light Community Board skin (the last contrast miss).
- **Ended opportunities.** A shared link to an entry that has closed will show what's still
  open in that country, instead of a dead end.

## Later

- **Optional accounts**, only once they're done properly: a secure sign-in, a profile, and a
  one-click *Delete my account*. Browsing and keeping a list will always work without one.
- **More coverage** where the map is still thin (South and Southeast Asia, Africa, parts of
  Latin America and Eastern Europe).

## Have an idea?

Open an [idea issue](../../issues/new/choose). Suggestions for sources or missing
opportunities are especially welcome.
