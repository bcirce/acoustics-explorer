# Changelog

What changed in each version, in plain language.

## 2.0.0 — upcoming · going public

A new home: **acousticsexplorer.org**.

- **330+ opportunities** in 55+ countries, including a full round of 2027 master's
  intakes, a jobs sweep and congresses into late 2027.
- **No account, no cookies.** Browsing, keeping a list and submitting all work without
  signing up, and visitor sign-in is switched off until a properly secure one is ready.
- **Saving made clearer.** The first time you save an entry, a short note explains that your
  list stays on this device only.
- **Nothing loaded from third parties** except the map tiles: fonts and country shapes are
  now served by the site itself.
- **Stricter entries.** Every entry must meet a written minimum, and a job with no deadline
  that nobody has confirmed for 60 days is hidden until someone checks it.
- **Easier to read.** Dimmed sidebar text (chip counts, small labels) raised above 4.5:1
  contrast in every skin.
- **Map credits.** © OpenStreetMap contributors and © CARTO, readable on the map.
- **Real "not found" answers.** A link to an entry or country that doesn't exist now returns
  a proper 404, so search engines don't index dead pages.
- **Our own error pages.** A friendly "Nothing here" page for broken links and a "Something
  went wrong" page with a *Try again* button, both showing the HTTP status code (404, 500)
  for the engineers among us.
- **Clearer privacy page**, written for Brazil's LGPD, with every processor, transfer and
  retention period listed.
- **Legacy skin retired.** Seven skins remain; anyone who had Legacy saved now gets Anechoic.
- **A new credit mark** in the corner, drawn by the author at age five.
- **Phone fixes.** No more stray focus rings on the menu button or the filters panel after a
  tap (Safari and Firefox on iPhone).

## 1.9.0 — 26 Sep 2026

- The status (soon, open, closed) is now written as a word on every result row.
- Bigger touch targets on phones: small icon buttons get an invisible 44px tap area.
- VoiceOver fixes: the filters sheet is named, chip counts are read aloud, and card header
  buttons are easier to hit.
- Animations follow the system's reduced-motion setting everywhere.

## 1.8.0 — 20 Sep 2026 · the phone release

- A redesigned phone experience: results as a swipeable deck, the filters as a sheet, and
  fixes found on real iPhones.

## 1.7.0 — 18 Sep 2026

- **My list:** bookmark entries on your device, no account needed.
- Filters rebuilt as chips with live counts.
- A large round of new opportunities from society feeds.

## 1.6.0 — 18 Sep 2026

- Submit an opportunity without creating an account.
- Polish for the map-behind layout on phones.

## 1.5.0 — 18 Jul 2026

- The map now fills the whole phone screen, with results in a bottom sheet you can drag.
- Search comes first in the phone sidebar.

## 1.4.0 — 18 Jul 2026 · the map-behind era

- Every skin now floats its panels over a full-screen map.
- Opening an entry flies the map to its city; closing it brings you back.

## 1.3.0 — 18 Jul 2026

- **Community submissions** with moderation, and sign-in by email link.
- Shareable links for every country and entry.

## Before 1.3

The first interactive map, the move from sample data to a real database of researched
entries, and the first skins.
